About

I'm an investment banker focused on energy transition, power, and digital infrastructure, working across M&A, project finance, and offtake advisory in North American and European markets.

I got into this work through an unlikely door. At Whitman College, learning that our endowment was invested in local wind farms sent me down a rabbit hole I never really climbed out of. I started at Onyx Renewable Partners, learning project finance from the developer side and closing solar and storage deals across the US. From there I moved to Marathon Capital to go bigger: utility-scale transactions, international markets, a wider range of clients and structures.

Over the last few years I've built a lot of my work around European deals, including power assets in the Nordics, data centre infrastructure, and cross-border capital raises. I'm now relocating to London to keep building in that direction.

Outside of work I ski as much as I possibly can. I raced in college and still try to get into the backcountry whenever I'm able. Last fall I climbed Mont Blanc. I'm working toward my private pilot's license, slowly but surely.
